# NGHE Autonomous Construction Scenario (medium)

> Input text that realizes all ground-truth information of the ground truth `nghe.gold.json` at the level of an operational document. The medium difficulty mixes referring expressions, omitted subjects, and some qualitative time expressions, but preserves all information.

The control center set the goal of digging out more than 38,000 m3 within the first 72 h while keeping personnel accidents at zero. On the day before deployment, the maintenance team first laid out the BSS_T battery swap station, the energy storage and supplementary charging facilities, and the packs that had finished charging. At the same time, the site supervisor and the control center turned on the RTDD dedicated network and checked whether the latency was within 20 ms, whether encryption was 2 ms or less, and whether HSM authentication held up properly. The next morning, DMSS and ASPCS swept the terrain as a 3D point cloud and aligned the GPS-RTK reference points to create the BIM and digital twin model. 5 U-EEX excavators, 10 U-EDT dump trucks, 2 U-EDB bulldozers, and 2 U-ERC rollers, nineteen units in all, finished authentication and calibration simultaneously. Since it was the first operation, the remote supervisor approved starting with Level 3 supervised autonomy first.

When the indicators had been stable for about two hours, it was raised to Level 4 full autonomy. From then on, AI_ICP and the equipment ran excavation, hauling, grading, and compaction on their own, and the remote operator controlled the equipment fleet without direct operation. When the charge of U-EEX Unit 1 fell to an SoC of 15%, BSS_T swapped the pack within 5 min, and AI_ICP adjusted the sequence so that two or more units would not drop out at once. Meanwhile, an anomaly sign was caught in the hydraulics of U-EDT Unit 7, so the maintenance team replaced the module within 1 h, and on the onboard side the OTA route optimization patch was applied without interruption. Night work noise was kept at 65 dB or less.

Around 48 h after the start of construction, dense fog rolled in, visibility sank below 200 m, and sensor confidence dropped. On this basis, AI_ICP suggested lowering one level and the remote supervisor approved, so the operation came down from Level 4 to Level 3 restricted operation in fog, with speed held to 5 km/h or less and the safety distance widened two times. In that fog, the HSM of U-EEX Unit 3 caught that the digital signature of an incoming command packet was forged. Since it was an attempt to push commands in from outside, the security system cut off the public network, switched over to the internal closed network, and left a security threat log.

When the lens of U-EEX Unit 2 was covered more than 50% by mud and a worker who came over to clean it was caught as a pedestrian, only that excavator stopped first. That night, when heavy rain of 30 mm/h poured down as forecast, the remote supervisor imposed a forced stop, and all equipment that had been operating in Level 3 during fog stopped at Level 1 and withdrew to the evacuation points. When the rain stopped, DMSS and ASPCS swept the terrain again and set the reference points, and 2 subsided zones newly came to light. AI_ICP laid out the routes again to avoid those spots. After the site supervisor's inspection was over, the remote supervisor first resumed in Level 3, and after seeing it run fine for about 30 min, raised it to Level 4 again. Toward the end of the 72 h, the cumulative volume reached about 38,200 m3, exceeding the goal. The ICCC and AI_ICP automatically generated a comprehensive performance report including zero personnel accidents and wrapped up the mission.
