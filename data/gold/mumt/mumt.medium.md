# MUM-T Attack Maneuver Scenario (medium)

The tank company prepared for maneuver with a MUM-T system that combined reconnaissance Drone 1, Drone 2, and Drone 3 with the TDSS. First, the TDSS confirmed that communication with the battalion server was normal and checked the SA function. The three drones launched in turn and divided the forward area and both flanks among themselves. The company commander shared that surveillance status with the subordinate platoons, and the TDSS sent the company SA summary up to the battalion server.

During the maneuver, Drone 1 picked up a heat source beyond the ridge suspected to be an enemy observation post. Since the battalion analysis also judged that there was a high probability of anti-tank positions in that area, the company commander decided to bypass to the right. A little later, the Edge AI of the same drone caught three or four FPV kamikaze drones, and the TDSS raised an alert with the approach direction and roughly 43 s remaining until arrival. The company commander immediately ordered all platoons to disperse and keep air-defense watch, and the platoons immediately scattered and evaded.

Right after that, the enemy launched electronic warfare jamming, and as a result the packet loss rate stayed above the threshold. With this persistent loss as the condition, the TDSS moved from normal to limited communication mode. Because at least the survivability functions and minimum SA had to be kept, the three drones cut the video stream, switched to Edge analysis mode, and sent up only summary tracks. It was a graceful degradation that accepted the quality degradation. When the jamming grew stronger and even the heartbeat was cut off for a certain time, this time it transitioned from limited to blackout mode. As integrated command became difficult, it turned on the local sensor-based survival loop, and the drones spread threat coordinates directly with emergency broadcast messages. When Drone 3 caught an enemy ATGM launch and broadcast it, the 2nd Platoon laid a smoke screen and pulled out. Meanwhile, Drone 1 landed as its battery ran out, and the usable drones dropped from three to two.

The links of the remaining Drone 2 and Drone 3 began to come back. When they held stably for a certain time, it rose from blackout to limited, and it realigned SA with the summary tracks accumulated during the blackout. When bandwidth recovered and the connection stabilized, it returned from limited to normal. The company commander reported a summary of the blackout period to the battalion server. Tank combat power remained intact, and afterward the platoons resumed the attack toward the objective, suppressed the remaining enemy positions, and secured the objective.
