# MUM-T Attack Maneuver Scenario (easy)

> Input text that explicitly realizes all answer information in the ground truth `mumt.gold.json`. At the easy difficulty, the description corresponds almost 1:1 to the slots.

The tank company operates a MUM-T system composed of reconnaissance Drone 1, Drone 2, and Drone 3 and the TDSS. The TDSS confirms normal communication with the battalion server and checks the situational awareness (SA) function. The three reconnaissance drones launch sequentially and are deployed for forward and left and right flank surveillance. The company commander shares the surveillance status with the subordinate platoons, and the TDSS transmits the company SA summary information to the battalion server.

Drone 1 detects a suspected enemy observation post heat source behind the ridge. Because the battalion analysis result shows a high probability that enemy anti-tank positions are deployed on that ridge, the company commander decides on a right bypass maneuver. Next, the Drone 1 Edge AI detects the possibility of 3-4 FPV kamikaze drones. The TDSS displays the threat direction, approach speed, and estimated time of arrival of about 43 s on the HMI to provide an FPV threat alert. The company commander orders all platoons to conduct dispersed maneuver and air-defense watch, and the subordinate platoons evade the FPV drones.

As the enemy begins electronic warfare jamming, a state in which the packet loss rate exceeds the threshold persists. With the persistent packet loss as the direct condition, the TDSS transitions from normal communication to limited communication mode. To maintain survivability-focused functions and minimum SA, the three reconnaissance drones stop video stream transmission and switch to Edge AI analysis mode to transmit summary track data. This transition is a graceful degradation based on communication quality degradation. Next, when heartbeat loss persists for a certain time or more, the TDSS transitions from limited communication to communication blackout mode. Because integrated command is impossible, the TDSS activates the local sensor-based survival loop, and the three reconnaissance drones transmit drone emergency broadcast messages. When Drone 3 detects an enemy ATGM launch, the 2nd Platoon deploys a smoke screen and performs an evasive maneuver. Drone 1 lands because its battery is depleted, and the available drones decrease from 3 to 2.

When the links of the remaining Drone 2 and Drone 3 remain stable for a certain time or more, the TDSS transitions from communication blackout to limited communication and resynchronizes SA with summary track data. When bandwidth recovers and connection stability is maintained, the TDSS transitions from limited communication to normal communication. The company commander transmits a comprehensive report on the blackout period to the battalion server. Tank combat power remains maintained, and the subordinate platoons resume the attack maneuver, suppress the remaining enemy positions, and secure the objective.
