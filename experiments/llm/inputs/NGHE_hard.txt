[P1] The line drawn by the control side was clear. In the first 72 h, that is, within three days, dig out more than 38,000 m3, but hold personnel accidents at zero. The night before, the maintenance crew laid out the BSS_T swap facility, the storage and supplementary charging devices, and the fully charged battery packs first, and at the same hour the site supervisor and the ICCC brought up the RTDD dedicated network and checked whether the end-to-end latency was within 20 ms, whether the encryption latency was 2 ms or less, and whether HSM authentication held. At daybreak, DMSS and ASPCS swept the terrain with a 3D point cloud and aligned the GPS-RTK references to create the BIM and digital twin model. The 5 U-EEX excavators and 10 U-EDT dump trucks, 2 U-EDB bulldozers, and 2 U-ERC rollers, 19 units in total, finished authentication and calibration all at once. Because it was the first run, the remote supervisor had it open in Level 3 supervised autonomy.

[P2] After watching for about 2 h and the indicators settling down, it was raised to Level 4 full autonomy. From then on, AI_ICP and the equipment dug, hauled, graded, and compacted by themselves, and the remote operator watched them as a group without touching anything. When the charge of U-EEX Unit 1 touched an SoC of 15%, BSS_T changed the pack within 5 min, and AI_ICP reworked the sequence so that more than two would not drop out at once. Meanwhile, a hint of trouble was caught in the hydraulics of U-EDT Unit 7, so the maintenance team swapped in the module within 1 h, and the onboard system even loaded the OTA route optimization patch while still rolling. Even at night, noise stayed at 65 dB or less.

[P3] About 48 h after the start of construction, fog settled in. The range of sight narrowed to 200 m or less and sensor confidence wavered too. AI_ICP put forward lowering it and the remote supervisor accepted, so the operation went down one level from Level 4 to Level 3 restricted operation in fog, holding speed to 5 km/h or less and widening the safety distance two times as well. In that gap, the HSM of U-EEX Unit 3 caught a command packet carrying a fake digital signature. Since it was an attempt to push commands in from outside, the security system cut off the public network open to the outside, retreated into the internal closed security network, and left a security threat log.

[P4] When the eyes of U-EEX Unit 2, that is, the sensor lens, were covered 50% or more by mud and the maintenance worker who came to wipe it was caught as a pedestrian, that one unit stopped first. Then, when heavy rain of 30 mm/h poured down at night, the remote supervisor issued a forced stop command to take hands off, and all equipment that had been moving in Level 3 during fog withdrew into the Level 1 emergency stop and safe evacuation state. Only after it stopped did DMSS and ASPCS sweep again and set the references, and 2 ground subsidence zones newly came to light. AI_ICP laid out the routes again to steer around those spots. When the site supervisor's safety inspection was over, the remote supervisor first returned it to Level 3 supervised autonomy, and after seeing it run without trouble for about 30 min, raised it to Level 4 full autonomy. At the tail end of the 72 h, the accumulated volume was about 38,200 m3, slightly exceeding the goal of 38,000 m3. The ICCC and AI_ICP automatically produced a comprehensive performance report including zero personnel accidents and closed the mission.
