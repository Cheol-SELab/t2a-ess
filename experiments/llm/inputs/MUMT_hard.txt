[P1] Reconnaissance UAVs 1, 2, and 3 and the decision support system TDSS moved bound together as one body. First, that system looked into whether its connection with the battalion server was sound and checked the situational awareness, that is, the SA function. Then, as the three rose in turn and split the front and the left and right among themselves, the commander passed that surveillance status down to the subordinates, and the TDSS sent the company SA summary upward.

[P2] It was Drone 1 that first caught a suspicious heat source behind the ridge. Since the higher-echelon analysis also said that anti-tank positions were likely laid out over there, the commander chose to swing to the right. Soon the Edge AI of the same Drone 1 filtered out three or four small FPV kamikaze craft, and the system raised an alert that, in addition to the approach direction and speed, about 43 s, that is, 17 s shorter than 1 min, remained until arrival. The commander told them without delay to scatter and watch the sky, and the subordinates spread out just so and evaded.

[P3] Then the other side began to shake the airwaves, and because of that the lost packets stayed past the line. With exactly that persistent loss as the condition, the TDSS sank from normal communication to limited communication. Because staying alive and keeping minimum SA came first, the three airframes folded up the video stream, turned to Edge AI analysis mode, and sent up only summary track data. It was, in effect, a graceful degradation that gave up image quality and kept the functions. When the shaking grew fiercer and even the heartbeat was cut off for a certain time, this time it passed from limited to communication blackout. As commanding everything as one became difficult, the TDSS turned on the survival loop that relied on local sensing, and the three drones spread threat coordinates with emergency broadcast messages without going through the company commander's tank. Of them, when Drone 3 caught and announced an anti-tank guided missile, that is, ATGM, launch, the 2nd Platoon laid a smoke screen and withdrew. Meanwhile, Drone 1, which had first watched the heat source and the FPVs, came down as its battery hit bottom, and the usable drones dropped from 3 to 2.

[P4] From the remaining Drone 2 and Drone 3, the signals that had been cut came back sporadically. When they held stably for a certain time, it rose from blackout to limited, and it realigned SA with the summary track data stored up while away. When bandwidth revived and the connection firmed up, it came back from limited to normal. The commander sent the comprehensive report on the period of the cutoff up to the battalion server. Tank combat power stayed as it was, and the subordinates pushed in toward the objective again, pressed down the remaining positions, and secured the objective.
