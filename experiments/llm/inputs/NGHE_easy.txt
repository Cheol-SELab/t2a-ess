[P1] The integrated control center (ICCC) sets the goal of completing at least 38,000 m3 of earthwork during the first 72 h and keeping personnel accidents at zero. The site maintenance team installs the battery swap station (BSS_T), the energy storage system, the supplementary charging system, and fully charged battery packs. The site supervisor and the ICCC confirm that the end-to-end latency of the RTDD dedicated network is within 20 ms, that the encryption latency is 2 ms or less, and that HSM authentication is normal. DMSS and ASPCS scan the 3D point cloud terrain data and correct the GPS-RTK reference points to create the BIM and digital twin model. The unmanned equipment fleet of 19 units, consisting of 5 U-EEX excavators, 10 U-EDT dump trucks, 2 U-EDB bulldozers, and 2 U-ERC rollers, performs authentication and calibration simultaneously. Because this is the first operation, the remote supervisor approves starting in Level 3 supervised autonomy mode.

[P2] When all indicators stay within the normal range for about 2 h, the transition to Level 4 full autonomy mode is approved. AI_ICP and the unmanned equipment fleet autonomously start the processes of excavation by U-EEX, hauling by U-EDT, soil grading by U-EDB, and compaction by U-ERC. The remote operator controls the equipment fleet but does not operate it directly. When the battery SoC of U-EEX Unit 1 reaches 15%, BSS_T replaces the battery pack within 5 min. AI_ICP optimizes the swap sequence so that two or more units do not leave work at the same time. When the PHM module of AI_ICP detects an anomaly sign in the pressure pattern of the hydraulic system of U-EDT Unit 7, the maintenance team, on this basis, performs preventive maintenance on the hydraulic module of U-EDT Unit 7 within 1 h. The onboard system applies the OTA route optimization patch, and night work noise is kept at 65 dB or less.

[P3] About 48 h after the start of construction, dense fog occurs, visibility drops to 200 m or less, and the sensor confidence score falls. AI_ICP proposes a downgrade to Level 3 and the remote supervisor approves it, so the operation transitions from Level 4 to the Level 3 restricted operation in fog state. Equipment speed is limited to 5 km/h or less and the safety distance is expanded 2 times. In the fog, the HSM of U-EEX Unit 3 detects a forged digital signature on a received command packet. On the basis of the forged command packet injection attempt, the security system blocks the external public network, switches to the internal closed security network, and generates a security threat log.

[P4] When the sensor lens of U-EEX Unit 2 is covered 50% or more by sticky soil and a maintenance worker approaches, the unit detects a pedestrian and performs a local stop. Then, when the heavy rain intensifies to 30 mm/h, the remote supervisor issues a forced work stop command, and all equipment that was operating in Level 3 during fog transitions to the Level 1 emergency stop and safe evacuation state. When the heavy rain ends, DMSS and ASPCS rescan the terrain and re-correct the reference points, and 2 ground subsidence zones are identified. AI_ICP recalculates the routes to bypass the subsidence zones. After the site supervisor completes the safety inspection, the remote supervisor resumes construction in Level 3, and when about 30 min of normal operation is confirmed, switches back to Level 4. At the final 72 h point, the cumulative earthwork volume is about 38,200 m3, meeting the goal of 38,000 m3. The ICCC and AI_ICP automatically generate a comprehensive performance report including zero personnel accidents and complete the mission.
